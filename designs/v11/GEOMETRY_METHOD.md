# v11 geometry method and continuity limits

v11 keeps the dense rounded web while giving its guide curves, ring profiles and strand roots explicit mathematical construction. **Smooth guide curves do not establish an exactly smooth final mesh or a structurally qualified joint.** The editable construction is in [veil_v11.py](source/veil_v11.py) and [junction_blend.py](source/junction_blend.py), with the crossing stage in [blend_crossings.py](source/blend_crossings.py).

## Periodic boundary guides

The rim radius, base radius and rim height are sampled around the console and interpolated with SciPy `CubicSpline(..., bc_type='periodic')`. The endpoint value is repeated to close the period. This makes the interpolated scalar guides twice continuously differentiable, including matched first and second derivatives at the periodic seam. [SciPy's CubicSpline documentation](https://docs.scipy.org/doc/scipy/reference/generated/scipy.interpolate.CubicSpline.html) describes this C2 property and the periodic boundary conditions.

The input radius/height samples are smoothed before interpolation. The rim fitting iteratively adds a smoothed local clearance deficit, then a small residual offset. The base envelope is built independently from the compact v10 reference so a local rim adjustment does not inflate the whole footprint. Some preparatory sample arrays use pointwise bounds, but the final periodic cubic interpolant—not that raw bounded array—is the evaluated guide. Cubic interpolation can still overshoot between samples: preserving an input clearance target is not proof of complete clearance. The final Boolean cavity cut and exported-mesh intersection checks are therefore separate requirements.

For circumferential parameter θ and normalized height t, the guide surface is:

```text
r(θ,t) = r_rim(θ) + [r_base(θ) − r_rim(θ)](1 − t)²
z(θ,t) = 2 + t[h(θ) − 4.4]
x = 8 + r cos θ
y = r sin θ
```

The base guide is at t=0 and z=2 mm. The upper guide is at t=1 and z=h−2.4 mm. This surface guides the weave; it is not a solid liner.

## Strand endpoints and rings

Two circumferential strands and 66 rising strands retain the dense asymmetric web. Along each rising strand, v runs from 0 to 1 and its guide height uses the quintic easing function:

```text
t(v) = 6v⁵ − 15v⁴ + 10v³
```

Its first and second derivatives vanish at both ends. Consequently, where the angular derivative is nonzero, the ideal strand centerline approaches the local ring tangent instead of crossing it at an arbitrary angle. Numerical sampling and the swept section frame remain additional sources of error.

The strand's nominal midspan radius is 1.65 mm. Separate quintic ramps increase it to 3 mm at the base ring and 2.4 mm at the upper ring. These are deliberate root enlargements, not the earlier v7 stress-based collar. The circular sweeps use 48 section vertices. The 3 mm-radius base ring is centered at z=2 mm and clipped at the desk plane, leaving a flat contact face and a 5 mm overall vertical span. The upper ring uses radius 2.4 mm. Desk clipping intentionally introduces a contact edge.

## Local root blending

Each rising strand has a dedicated patch at both rings. The rest of the model retains its swept CAD construction; there is no global voxel remesh.

Within a root neighborhood, the script approximates distances d1 and d2 to sampled, variable-radius strand and ring tubes. The corrected polynomial blend is:

```text
h = clamp[0.5 + 0.5(d2 − d1)/k, 0, 1]
f = (1 − h)d2 + h d1 − k h(1 − h)
```

The blend parameter k reaches 0.85 mm in the patch interior and tapers over 1.2 mm to a zero-blend outer margin of 0.5 mm. Its maximum distance-field offset from the hard union is k/4, or 0.2125 mm at full blend; this is not a guarantee of that exact geometric surface displacement everywhere.

The local zero surface is sampled at 0.20 mm grid spacing and triangulated by marching cubes. Patches are added to the swept skin with a Boolean union. This adds material around the root rather than globally smoothing away strand details. Local build caches `preblend_skin.stl` and `guide_curves.npz` preserve comparison geometry and sampled guides; `junction_blend.json` records the patch parameters. The large caches are regenerated by the source and are not included in Git.

## Middle-web crossings

A second stage detects nearby strand pairs away from the upper and lower rings, groups nearby candidate intersections, and adds smaller local patches to the cached `root_only.stl`. This uses the same corrected blend formula with k=0.45 mm and a 0.12 mm sampling pitch. The maximum scalar-field offset is 0.1125 mm. The blend tapers over 1.2 mm and is zero within a 0.5 mm boundary margin. `crossing_blend.json` records the selected locations and added volume.

This is a targeted proximity-based crossing treatment, not proof that every possible crossing or every three-way intersection has an exact fillet. The final cavity is cut again after these additions. Root-only and preblend geometry remain local build caches to distinguish the two stages; they are regenerated rather than committed to Git. Final exports must be checked independently after all additions.

## Export topology repair

The crossing-union export contained microscopic sliver edges after coordinate quantization. An independent diagnostic welded 11 near-duplicate edge pairs with a maximum vertex movement of 0.00000815 mm, removed 15 resulting index-degenerate faces and changed volume by 0.000000134 mm³. The resulting mesh was one watertight component with consistent winding; see `analysis/export_sliver_review.json`.

The independently repaired full-precision crossing cache was then simplified at 0.004 mm and exported through `recover_crossing_export.py`. The final body and coupon passed float64 and float32 topology checks; `output/export_diagnostics.json` records those export stages. The exporter also includes a bounded sliver-weld fallback for rebuilding. This is a narrowly constrained topology cleanup, not global smoothing or generic hole filling. The delivered body is one watertight component with consistent winding; STL and 3MF agree.

## What continuity is—and is not—established

- **C2 periodic scalar guide curves:** supplied by the chosen spline construction and subject to numerical verification.
- **Ideal endpoint centerline tangency:** designed through the quintic height law and ring-based guide surface; sampled tangent measurements can check its approximation.
- **Locally blended root shape:** produced by the distance-field patch and Boolean union. The tube distances use sampled polyline segments, and patch extraction has finite resolution.
- **No blanket G1/G2 surface claim:** neither smooth centerlines nor the blend formula establishes tangent-plane or curvature continuity across every final patch boundary, wire crossing, cavity cut or desk edge. The exported STL/3MF is triangulated and has planar facets.

Display smoothing may interpolate normals without changing the mesh. Visual review must therefore include flat-shaded close-ups alongside normal full-model renders. Mesh connectedness and watertightness are separate from continuity, printability and strength. No FEA has been run for v11; historical v7 results do not apply to this geometry.

## Final independent checks

The final exported body is 151.714 × 244.556 × 53.218 mm and 128.409 cm³. The inherited console-plan intersection is 0 mm³; the nominal-clearance-envelope intersection is approximately 0.0000126 mm³ at numerical/export scale. These checks concern the reference envelope, not measured hardware fit.

Seventy-four sampled root-patch boundary points had median/P95/maximum distance to the preblend surface of approximately 0.00173 / 0.00690 / 0.00845 mm. This bounds those samples only; it is not a global continuity certificate. One representative crossing was recomputed at 0.06 mm pitch versus the delivered 0.12 mm pitch: added fillet volume changed from 1.23238 to 1.23372 mm³ (about 0.108%). That is a local resolution check, not convergence proof for all 305 crossings.

The crossing stage added 416.317 mm³ to the unsimplified Boolean body. Comparing the exported root-only and final meshes gives 407.051 mm³; the figures differ because export/simplification also changes geometry. Do not label the unsimplified addition as the exact delivered volume increase.

Evidence is in `analysis/independent_review.json`, `preflight.json`, `root_band_check.json`, `crossing_resolution_review.json` and `final_roundtrip.json`. The 132 root patches and 305 crossing patches improve the local transition design, but sampled construction, Boolean operations and triangulation still preclude a blanket exact G1/G2 claim. No FEA or physical qualification was performed for v11.
