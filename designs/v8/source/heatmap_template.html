Engineering screening, not proof of safety. Material is assumed isotropic (E = 1,200 MPa, ν = 0.35). Feet are constrained. Printed layer strength, creep, impacts, true contact and vent clearance are not validated. Peak stresses at sharp corners or constraints may be mesh-sensitive. Whole-stand simulation geometry is regularized: about 1.15% less volume, 0.35 mm sampled P95 surface error and 1.84 mm sampled maximum error. Fine strand notches are not resolved reliably; this is not a no-weak-points guarantee.
Load and support assumptions

Vertical: 31.392 N over rib-top faces inside the reference core footprint. Eccentric: the same force on the portion of contact with x > 8 mm (approximately the +X half). Where included, the 10 N side case acts on a local upper-shell patch, not at the top of a modeled console. The console itself is not meshed.

How to read the view

Each boundary triangle is colored with its adjacent tetrahedron's computed stress. Deformation uses solved nodal displacement and the explicit exaggeration factor. Compare variants with the common scale. No color denotes a certified allowable stress. This file is self-contained and makes no network requests.
