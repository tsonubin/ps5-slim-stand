# Print-service brief / 打印服务说明

**Development prototype, not a certified console support. Please quote; do not proceed to the full load-bearing part without prototype review.**

- Part: one-piece PS5 Slim Disc stand, white or blue. Size 151.30 × 244.18 × 63.35 mm. Geometry only; 3MF has no machine/material profile. STL units are mm; do not automatically scale.
- Quote suitable FDM material and, if available, a powder-bed nylon process separately. Please state process/material grade, tolerances, layer height, surface finish and expected creep under sustained warm loading. Material choice is not yet qualified.
- For an FDM feasibility slice, start upright as exported, approximately 0.4 mm nozzle / 0.16–0.20 mm layers / four perimeters; these are review starting points, not validated production settings. Ensure thin members are actually filled. Use a brim if needed, without shrinking the cavity.
- Nominal members: 2.6 mm lattice width, 2.4 mm lattice depth, 3.2 mm ribs; 1.5 mm nominal reference clearance. Check the actual slicer preview for omitted walls, isolated extrusion, roof bridging and rim overhangs. Review z≈25.5, 39.9, 48.5 and 58.7 mm. Supports may be necessary; show how they can be removed without breaking the lattice.
- First print `GAUGE_v6_cavity_1.6mm.stl` and `PRINT_TEST_v6_section.stl` using the proposed process. The gauge is 106.9 × 227.0 × 1.6 mm (~3.77 cm³); the test section is 29.6 × 32 × 48.5 mm (~1.76 cm³). Keep the test section upright with its flat base on the plate. It covers only part of the stand's printing challenges.
- Please return the slice preview, support layout, material amount and photos of the test section. Keep the cavity dimensions and support height intact during finishing. Do not glue broken lattice struts and present the result as an intact production part.
- Full prototype: inspect flatness/rocking, layer bonding and all junctions; verify console contact on the black core without loading white covers. Check rear plugs, cable bends, disc insertion and every vent. Any protective pads change seat height/clearance and must be included in the fit check. Perform load/deformation testing with a restrained dummy mass before using the console.

## 可直接发给淘宝 / 阿里巴巴商家

这是 PS5 Slim 光驱版支架的**研发样件**，不是已经验证承重和散热的成品。请先报价和审核工艺，不要直接按量产件处理。

1. 尺寸约 151.30 × 244.18 × 63.35 mm，一体打印，白色或蓝色。STL 按毫米、100% 导入；3MF 仅含几何模型，不含打印参数。请勿自动缩放。
2. 请说明可用材料牌号、工艺、精度、层高、表面处理及持续受载和温热环境下的适用性。可分别提供 FDM 和尼龙粉末烧结类工艺报价，材料尚未最终验证。
3. FDM 请按模型直立方向进行切片评估。0.4 mm 喷嘴、0.16–0.20 mm 层高、四道墙仅供初始评估，需由贵方调整。最细格栅名义尺寸约 2.6 × 2.4 mm，内部肋厚 3.2 mm。请检查薄壁是否漏打、悬空路径、跨桥、顶部边缘和支撑拆除方式，不能默认免支撑。
4. 请先打印轮廓量规和小段测试件，采用拟用于成品的同一工艺。量规只检查轮廓，不能承重。请提供切片预览、支撑方案、耗材估算和样件照片。重点查看高度约 25.5 / 39.9 / 48.5 / 58.7 mm 的局部悬垂。
5. 成品样件需另行检查平整度、层间结合、机身黑色底部接触、接口/光盘/线缆及通风孔避让，并用受约束的模拟配重做承重和变形测试，再考虑放置主机。尚未做实际装机、强度和散热验证。
